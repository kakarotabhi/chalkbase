# Manage guardians

The parents and guardians of the children at your school, as one list.

**Who can do this:** the principal, the vice principal and the admission counsellor can add and edit
guardians. Class teachers and the accountant can see them.

## One parent, one record

A father with three children at this school should be **one** entry linked to all three — not three
entries that happen to have the same name.

This is the single most important habit on this screen, and the reason is practical rather than
tidy. When there is one record, correcting his phone number once corrects it for all three children.
When there are three, you fix the one in front of you, the other two keep the old number, and
nothing tells you they disagree. You find out when the school rings about an absence and nobody
answers.

## Before you add anyone, search

Search by **phone number**. It is the thing that reliably tells two people apart when families share
a surname, and it works however the number was typed — with spaces, with or without `+91`, it makes
no difference. Typing the last ten digits is enough.

If the parent is already here, use that record. Only add a new one when the search genuinely finds
nobody.

If you start typing a phone number into a new guardian's details and someone already has it, the
screen says so and offers you that person instead. It will not stop you — two people really can
share a number, a husband and wife most obviously — but read what it says before continuing.

## Checking whether two records are the same person

Select **Linked to N students** on any row and it opens the list: each child's name, admission
number, and which class and section they are in.

That is usually enough to tell two similar records apart — one Suresh has children in Class 3 and
Class 7, the other has one child in Class 11. A child who has left the school still appears here,
which is intentional: you are asking "is this the same person?", not "who is here now".

## Editing

Select a guardian to change their name, phone, email or occupation. The change applies everywhere
they are linked, immediately.

## Removing

There is no delete. To take a guardian off a child, open **that child's** record and use **Remove
from this student** — it removes the link, not the person, who may still be a parent to other
children here.

## What is recorded

Every change goes to the audit log: who edited a guardian and when. A guardian edit is recorded once
against that guardian, not once per child, because it was one change to one person.
