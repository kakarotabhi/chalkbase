# Read the dashboard

What is going on at your school this morning, in one screen.

**Who can do this:** everyone who signs in. What you see on it depends on your own role — a
principal and an auditor see different tiles, because each tile needs the same permission its own
screen does.

## Steps

1. Sign in. If your menu has anything on it at all, this is the first screen you land on.
2. Read the tiles that appear for you:
   - **Academic session** — which year the school is currently in, and whether one has been set at
     all. If nothing has been set yet, this says so instead of hiding the tile.
   - **Students enrolled** — how many students hold a live placement this session, and how many are
     in each class.
   - **Guardians and students without each other** — how many enrolled students have nobody on
     record to call, and how many guardians in the directory are attached to no student at all. Both
     numbers are things to fix, not just to know: open **Students** or **Guardians** and search for
     the gap.
   - **Recent activity** — the five most recent rows from the audit log, for whoever can open that
     log. Open **Audit log** from the menu for the rest.

## Why your dashboard looks different from someone else's

Every tile is switched on or off by the same permission that guards the screen behind it. Someone
holding only the Auditor role sees just **Recent activity** — nothing about students or the
session, because that role was never given permission to see student data. A class teacher sees
enrolment and the two gaps, but nothing about audit activity, for the matching reason.

This is not a bug and there is no way to see "everything" from here that your role could not already
open elsewhere. If a tile is missing that you think you should have, ask whoever manages roles at
your school to add the matching permission — "View academic sessions", "View students", "View
guardians", or "View the audit log".

## What is not here

There is no tile for fees, attendance, exams, or staff, because those parts of Chalkbase do not
exist yet. A tile only appears once there is a real number behind it — a count nobody could act on
would just be decoration.
