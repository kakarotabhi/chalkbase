# Approve attendance corrections

Once a day's attendance is more than a day old it locks, so a teacher who needs to fix a mark files
a correction request instead of editing it directly. This is where those requests are decided.

**Who can do this:** the principal and the vice principal.

## Review the queue

1. Open **Attendance → Corrections** from the menu. Every request awaiting a decision is listed,
   oldest first: the student, the date, what the mark currently says, what the teacher wants it
   changed to, and their reason.
2. Select **Approve** to accept the change, or **Reject** to leave the mark as it is. Either way you
   may add a short note — this is what the teacher sees back.

Approving changes the mark immediately. There is nothing further to do; the teacher's screen updates
to show the new status.

## What is recorded

Every request and its decision goes into the audit log — who requested it, who decided it, and when
— alongside the original mark's own record. Nothing here overwrites what was already logged: the
audit log keeps both the original mark and the correction, whichever way you decide.
