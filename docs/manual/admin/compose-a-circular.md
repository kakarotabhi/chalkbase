# Compose a circular

Send a notice to the families of one or more classes or sections, and see who it reached.

**Who can do this:** principals and vice principals. Class teachers can see circulars and record
that a family acknowledged one, but composing and publishing is not on their role by default.

## Compose and publish

1. Open **Communication → Circulars** from the menu.
2. Select **Compose a circular**.
3. Write a title and the message. Turn on **Ask families to acknowledge this circular** if you need
   to know that each family actually saw it — a PTM notice or a permission slip, for example.
4. Under **Targets**, choose a class. Leave the section as **Whole class** to reach every section of
   it, or choose one section to reach only that one. The number of students that target would reach
   is shown as soon as you choose it. Select **Add a target** for more than one class or section.
5. Select **Save as draft**. Nothing has gone out yet — a draft can be discarded and composed again
   if the audience is wrong.
6. When it is ready, open the draft and select **Publish**. This is final: the circular is recorded
   as delivered to every student the targets resolve to, and the targets cannot be changed
   afterwards.

## What "delivered" means today

There is no parent app to receive this yet. Publishing records exactly who a circular was meant to
reach — one row per student — which is what makes the recipient counts and the acknowledgement
tracking below real numbers rather than a guess. When a parent app exists, this is the record it
will read from; nothing about how you compose a circular today will need to change for that to
happen.

## Recording an acknowledgement

If a family tells you by phone, in writing, or in person that they have seen a circular that asks
for acknowledgement, open the circular and select **Record acknowledgement** next to that student.
Add a note if it helps (how they let you know, or anything they said), and save. This is recorded
against you, not invented on the family's behalf — see the audit log if you need to check who
recorded which acknowledgement and when.

## If you cannot see this screen at all

You need the "View circulars" permission to see the list, and "Compose and publish circulars" to
write one — both of which your school's Principal and Vice Principal roles hold by default. If
either is missing, ask whoever manages roles and access at your school to add it.
