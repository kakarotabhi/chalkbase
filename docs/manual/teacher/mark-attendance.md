# Mark attendance

Your class's attendance for the day, marked from your phone in the time it takes to read the roll.

**Who can do this:** class teachers, for the section they teach, and the principal or vice
principal as a stand-in. Subject teachers can see attendance but cannot mark it yet — that arrives
with the period-wise screen, which is not built.

## Mark today's register

1. Open **Attendance → Mark attendance** from the menu.
2. Choose your section if it is not already selected. Today's date is chosen for you.
3. Select **Mark all present**. Every student not yet marked is set to present.
4. Go down the list and change anyone who is not present: absent, late, half day, on excused leave,
   or — for a day the school is closed — holiday. Add a note if it helps you remember why later.
5. Select **Save**. The whole register is saved in one go.

You can come back and change anything you saved today, or edit yesterday's register, right up until
it locks. Nothing is lost if you close the screen partway through and come back — whatever you
already saved is still there.

## When the register is locked

A day's attendance locks automatically once it is more than a day old — end of that school day, plus
24 hours. After that you cannot change a mark directly; you will see a lock notice next to it
instead, with a **Request a correction** action. If a day was never marked at all, there is nothing
to correct — the banner says so plainly, and that day can no longer be marked.

1. Select **Request a correction** next to the student whose mark needs fixing.
2. Choose what it should have been, and say why in a sentence or two — this goes to whoever approves
   it, so be specific ("marked absent by mistake, she was at the school trip").
3. Submit. The mark does not change yet: it changes only once your principal or vice principal
   approves the request, and you will see the outcome on the same screen.

A locked mark is not a mistake you cannot fix — it just needs someone else to agree it was wrong,
so the register stays trustworthy for anyone reading it later.

## What is recorded

Every mark you save, and every correction request and its decision, goes into the audit log: who
did it and when, and which fields changed. The original mark is never erased when a correction is
approved — both stay on record.

## If you cannot see this screen at all

You need the "Mark attendance" permission, which your school's Class Teacher role holds by default.
If it is missing, ask whoever manages roles and access at your school to add it.
