# Capture an enquiry

A walk-in, a phone call, a website message, a referral from an existing family, a campaign, or a
list somebody hands you on paper — every one of them becomes a row here, with someone named to
follow it up.

**Who can do this:** admission counsellors, and the principal or vice principal as a stand-in.

## Add an enquiry

1. Open **Admissions → Enquiries** from the menu.
2. Select **Add enquiry**.
3. Enter the child's name. Add their date of birth and the class they are interested in if you
   know them — it is fine to leave either blank for a walk-in who has not decided yet.
4. Enter the parent or guardian's name and phone number. Their email is optional.
5. Choose where this enquiry came from: walk-in, phone call, website, referral, campaign, or
   imported.
6. Choose who this enquiry is assigned to. This is not optional — every enquiry needs an owner from
   the moment it is captured, so it never sits unanswered because nobody was told about it.
7. Add a note if there is anything worth remembering from this first contact.
8. Select **Add enquiry**. You are taken straight to the enquiry's own page, which is due for its
   first follow-up today.

## Find an enquiry again

Use the search box on the enquiry list to find a family by the child's name, the parent's name, or
their phone number. The status, source and counsellor pills narrow the list further — useful for
"every enquiry I have not converted or lost yet" or "everything that came in from the website".

## What happens next

An enquiry does not sit still once it is captured — see
[work your follow-up queue](work-your-follow-up-queue.md) for what happens after today.

## If you cannot see this screen at all

You need the "View enquiries" permission to see the list, and "Manage enquiries" to add one or log
a follow-up. Your school's Admission Counsellor role holds both by default. If either is missing,
ask whoever manages roles and access at your school to add it.
