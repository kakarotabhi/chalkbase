# Work your follow-up queue

Every enquiry that is due for a follow-up today, or overdue, in one list — the thing to open first
thing in the morning instead of a paper register.

**Who can do this:** admission counsellors, for their own enquiries by default. The principal or
vice principal can also see every counsellor's queue.

## Open your queue

1. Open **Admissions → Follow-up queue** from the menu. It shows **My enquiries** by default —
   nobody else's, so it stays a short list you can actually get through.
2. Each row shows the child, the parent's name and phone number, the class they are interested in,
   and whether the follow-up is **due today** or **overdue**. Overdue rows are the ones to call
   first.
3. Select a child's name to open the enquiry and log what happened.
4. If you supervise more than one counsellor, switch **My enquiries** to **Every counsellor's** to
   see the whole school's queue.

## Log a follow-up

From an enquiry's own page:

1. Select the section headed **Log a follow-up**.
2. Write what happened — the call, the visit, what the family said.
3. Set the next date to follow up on. You have to give one unless you are closing the enquiry (see
   below) — an open enquiry with no next date would quietly disappear from everyone's queue, which
   is exactly what this screen exists to prevent.
4. If the family has decided, move the status: **In progress** for an ordinary update, **Converted**
   once they are going ahead, or **Lost** once they are not.
5. Select **Log follow-up**. The enquiry's page updates immediately, and the follow-up joins its
   history below — nobody can go back and change what was written once it is saved.

Once an enquiry is Converted or Lost it drops off every queue for good; there is nothing left to
follow up on it.

## Reassigning an enquiry

If an enquiry should belong to someone else — you are going on leave, or a colleague already knows
the family — select **Reassign** on the enquiry's own page and choose the new counsellor. This
changes who owns the follow-up; it does not touch the enquiry's status or its history.

## If you cannot see this screen at all

You need the "View enquiries" permission. Your school's Admission Counsellor role holds it by
default. If it is missing, ask whoever manages roles and access at your school to add it.
